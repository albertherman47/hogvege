# Romanian reference demo data

The app's optional starter dataset covers January 2025–October 2026 (22 monthly rows). Every row is tagged `estimated`: the app is showing a modeled household expense, not claiming to reproduce a real person's bills. Amounts are RON (lei). Load it with **„Referenciaadatok betöltése”** in the demo banner.

## Assumptions and sources

- **Water and sewer, Bucharest:** six cubic metres per month. Monthly total is the published combined per-m³ tariff multiplied by six. January–June 2025 uses Apa Nova's 2024 rate plus 19% VAT; July 2025–June 2026 uses its published VAT-inclusive 2025 tariff (7.07 + 3.73 lei/m³); July–October 2026 uses the published VAT-inclusive 2026 tariff (7.98 + 4.21 lei/m³). These are Bucharest tariffs, not Romania-wide prices. [Apa Nova 2024 tariff](https://www.apanovabucuresti.ro/informatii-utile/stiri/ajustare-tarifara-2024), [2025 tariff](https://www.apanovabucuresti.ro/informatii-utile/stiri/apa-nova-respinge-acuzatiile-privind-ilegalitatea-facturarii-apei-pluviale-si-reafirma-conformitatea-cu-legislatia-nationala-si-europeana), [2026 tariff](https://www.apanovabucuresti.ro/informatii-utile/tarife-si-facturi/tarife-pentru-consum-apa-si-canalizare).
- **Natural gas:** a seasonal household consumption profile (kWh/month) multiplied by 0.31 lei/kWh. This was the household maximum billed price through March 2026; the 2026–27 scheme continues price protections but calculates the final price from regulated components, so applying 0.31 to later rows is a simplified estimate, not an asserted supplier tariff. [OUG 6/2025](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/295558), [OUG 12/2026](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/308480).
- **Mobile:** one DIGI Mobil Optim 2 line, modeled as 10 lei/month from the published 1.68 EUR/month excluding VAT tariff, using a rounded 5 lei/EUR conversion and VAT. It is a sample plan rather than a market average. [Sibiu public contract, 30 June 2025](https://www.sibiu.ro/ro2/cheltuieli_publice/SPSPMADP_ctr._nr_.32_30_.06.2025.pdf).
- **Fuel:** 40 litres of standard petrol 95 per month times the Romanian monthly mean price. 2025 monthly values are reconstructed estimates; 2026 January–February are filled with estimates, March–September use monthly observations, and October is month-to-date as of 16 October 2026. [PretCarburant.ro monthly index and methodology](https://pretcarburant.ro/index-preturi-lunar), [2025 archive](https://pretcarburant.ro/preturi-carburanti-2025).
- **Rent:** a two-room Bucharest apartment. The September 2025 city benchmark (€556) is used as a Jan–Nov 2025 proxy; December 2025's published €534 benchmark is used from Dec 2025 onward, consistent with June 2026's €533 reported average. Converted at a rounded 5 lei/EUR; this is a city market benchmark, not an actual lease. [September 2025 report](https://www.bucharest.ro/articles/rent-prices-in-bucharest-in-september-2025-how-much-it-costs-to-rent-studios-2-or-3-room-apartments-553), [June 2026 report](https://www.bucharest.ro/articles/bucharest-real-estate-market-in-june-2026-how-rents-evolved-and-how-much-a-two-room-apartment-costs-2217).

These assumptions make the sample useful for trying the app's history and charts. For budgeting, replace the benchmarks with household bills and actual meter or fuel consumption.
